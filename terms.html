Draft — attorney review needed

This page is a plain-language draft. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and is not final. Please do not treat it as legal terms until it has been reviewed and approved. Draft prepared September 30, 2026.

About this website

This website gives general information about our services and prices. It is a beta preview and is shared for review, so content, prices, and features may change or contain mistakes. Please call us to confirm anything important.

Information only, not a contract

Nothing on this website is a funeral contract. Arrangements are made with a funeral director, who will go over them with you.

Prices and the service picker

“Starting at” prices come from our current General Price List. Cemetery and crematory charges, merchandise, cash advance items, and applicable taxes are separate.

The service picker is a planning tool. Its estimates are not a final bill or a contract. Please ask a director to confirm them.

If you need us now

If a death has just occurred, please call (415) 668-0077. Please call rather than write for anything urgent. During this beta preview the contact form is a demo and does not send messages.

Other companies’ services

The contact page shows a Google map and links to Google Maps. Those are run by Google and have their own terms.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When they are final, this page will show the date of the latest update.

Questions
