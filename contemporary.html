Service options

Memorial service

A gathering to honor and remember, without the body present — often held after cremation, in our chapel or another meaningful place.

Includes everything in direct interment or cremation, plus the use of one of our chapels or drawing rooms, up to 2 hours, for a Quiet Hour, visiting or a memorial service. Final interment is scheduled at a separate time.

Direct cremation

A simple cremation without a formal service beforehand. A memorial can be held later.

Covers the paperwork and authorizations, a local daytime ambulance, and care and limited preparation of your loved one. The price list shows $1,945 with a fiberboard cremation container, and pacemaker removal is an additional cost.

Price list details

Official wording and price from our General Price List, shown in English.

DIRECT INTERMENT OR CREMATION: $1,820
This service includes appropriate documents and authorizations, local daytime ambulance, care and limited preparation and general care of remains. If you want to arrange a direct cremation, you can use an alternative container. Alternative containers encase the body and can be made of materials like fiberboard or composition materials (with or without outside covering); the container we provide is of fiberboard. In addition, as above including container for cremation: $1,945. Direct Interment including minimum burial container: $2,820. Pacemaker removal: additional cost.
