What happens next

Four calm steps

  1. Call us, any hour

    Weekends and holidays included — there is no wrong time to call.

  2. A director answers

    A real person who will listen, answer your questions, and explain what happens next at your pace.

  3. We arrange the transfer into our care

    We bring your loved one to Evergreen Mortuary with dignity and respect.

  4. We meet to plan

    When you are ready, we meet in person to talk through your wishes and the options that fit your family.
