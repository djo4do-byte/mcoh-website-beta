Privacy

What this website does and does not do with your information, in plain language.

Draft — attorney review needed

This page is a plain-language draft. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and is not final. Please do not treat it as legal terms until it has been reviewed and approved. Draft prepared September 30, 2026.

No accounts

This website has no accounts or sign-in. As built today, its pages do not include advertising or analytics tools.

What stays in your browser

Your language choice is saved in your browser so the site can remember it.

The service picker keeps your selections in your browser while you use it. The picker does not send them to us. They are cleared when you close the browser tab.

The contact form

During this beta preview the contact form is a demo. It does not send or store what you type.

For anything urgent, please call (415) 668-0077.

Google Maps

The contact page shows a Google map and links to Google Maps. When you open that page, Google provides the map and handles information under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this page

We may update this page. When it is final, it will show the date of the latest update.
