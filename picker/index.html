Our history

McAvoy O’Hara Company is among San Francisco’s oldest funeral homes and one of California’s oldest independent funeral businesses. It was established about 1850 as Flanigan & Gallagher, later McAvoy & Gallagher, and then McAvoy, O’Hara & Company.

We remain family-owned: today Richard O’Hara and Dan O’Hara III, third-generation funeral directors, carry on a tradition of personal, unhurried care.

The City has recognized our long service to the community, including commendation on our 150th anniversary.

Preneed contract

Plan ahead — so your family isn’t left guessing

When a death occurs, many permanent decisions must be made within a few hours. Families often say they aren’t sure what their loved one would have wanted. A preneed conversation lets you decide in an atmosphere of quiet consultation.

  • Your wishes, known. Record your preferences — from chapel time to cremation — while there is time to think.
  • Less confusion for family. Fewer hurried choices for the people who will arrange your services.
  • One call when needed. A person you choose calls us; we follow the preferences you set.

Next you’ll choose the same service options as any arrangement. Your director will complete the preneed contract with you — nothing is final on this tablet alone.
