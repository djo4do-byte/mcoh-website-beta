René Henri Revel passed away on October 23, 2026, with his family by his side. René was born on February 9, 1951, in San Francisco, the eldest of four children of Gaston and Eula Revel. He was a loving brother to his two sisters, Michele Bisho (Dave) and Suzanne Peoples (Scott), to his brother, Maurice Revel (deceased), and to his dear sister-in-law, Carolyn Coe (Jim). Beloved husband to Maureen Revel for over 50 years. Treasured father to Dominique Uskert (Chris) and Henri Revel (Belén). Cherished “Boppie” to Desmond Uskert, Harry Uskert and Isabel Revel.

René was a lifelong San Franciscan attending Saint Monica Grammar School, Sacred Heart Cathedral Preparatory (Class of ’69), and St. Mary’s College (Class of ’73) where he earned a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration & Economics and was a standout rugby player. René joined the San Francisco Fire Department in 1975 and rose through the ranks holding many positions including Director of Training on Treasure Island. He retired as a Battalion Chief in 2005. At the behest of his father and two uncles, René also served as Managing Partner of the family-owned Embassy Hotel on Polk Street in San Francisco.

René enjoyed the outdoors: hiking, skiing and riding his bike. He loved spending time with his family at their country home in Sebastopol and at the family’s San Francisco residence. He had a big presence and a big heart, and a wonderful sense of humor. He was humble about his accomplishments, and always put family first. He will be deeply missed. We will think of him every day, cry a little and laugh a lot.

The Revel family extends its gratitude to René’s UCSF medical team for their comprehensive treatment and care.

Family and friends are invited to a Memorial Mass honoring René at Église Notre Dame des Victoires, 566 Bush Street, San Francisco, CA 94108, on Friday, November 13, 2026 at 10:30am.

In lieu of flowers, please consider donating in René’s honor to the San Francisco Firefighters Cancer Prevention Foundation, 1139 Mission Street, San Francisco, CA 94103.
