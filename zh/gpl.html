INTRODUCTION:

The goods and services shown below are those we can provide to our clients. You may choose only the items you desire. If legal or other requirements mean you must buy any item you do not specifically ask for, we will explain the reason in writing on the statement we provide describing the funeral goods and services you selected. When both casket and service are selected, we provide discounted choices for certain service selections. Prior to drafting any contract for goods and services, the responsible party of the decedents survivor who is handling funeral arrangements is entitled to receive a copy of any preneed agreement in the possession of the funeral establishment that has been signed and paid for, in full or in part, by or on behalf of the decedent.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICE FEE:

We charge a fee for our initial services. This fee is for our basic services and is already included with the price of our caskets. This same fee of $2,700 will be added to the cost of your funeral arrangements if you provide the casket. It includes such costs and services as availability for your initial inquiry and consultation, coordination and direction of Arrangements, Services and Disposition. Also included are the use of common facilities, equipment and necessary supplies for services and completion of the appropriate documents including California State registration of death, a county permit for disposition, and preparing of requests certified copies of the Original Certificate. No taxes, merchandise, limousine, cemetery or crematory fees or third-party charges are included unless clearly specified.

SELECTED SERVICES

The discounted services listed here are specially packaged for your convenience and are available with purchase of both our services and the casket for use in services. With the initial professional services, we are available for your initial inquiry as well as for consultation, coordination and direction of Arrangements, Services and Disposition. Also included are the local ambulance call with attendant, sheltering and preparation of the remains as directed, the use of common facilities, equipment and necessary supplies for services and the completion of necessary documents including California State registration of death, the county permit for disposition and preparing requests for certified copies of the Original Certificate. Please note that items may be added to these ‘packaged’ selections, however substitutions and deletions are disallowed. No taxes or merchandise, limousine, cemetery, crematory or third-party charges are included unless clearly indicated.

Prior to drafting any contract for goods or services, the responsible party or the decedent’s survivor who is handling the arrangements is entitled to receive a copy of any preneed agreement in the possession of the funeral establishment that has been signed and paid for in full or in part, by or on behalf of the decedent.

DIRECT INTERMENT OR CREMATION: $ 1,820

This service includes appropriate documents and authorizations, local daytime ambulance, care and limited preparation and general care of remains.
