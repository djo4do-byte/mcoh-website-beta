Who we are

Why McAvoy O’Hara?

McAvoy O’Hara Company is among San Francisco’s oldest funeral homes and one of California’s oldest independent funeral businesses. It was established about 1850 as Flanigan & Gallagher, later McAvoy & Gallagher, and then McAvoy, O’Hara & Company.

We remain family-owned: today Richard O’Hara and Dan O’Hara III, third-generation funeral directors, carry on a tradition of personal, unhurried care.

Our 150th anniversary in 2000 was honored by a California State Senate resolution, and in 2003 Mayor Willie Brown recognized Dan and Richard O’Hara for helping design a comprehensive funeral service disclosure law.
