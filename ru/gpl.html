GENERAL PRICE LIST

INTRODUCTION:

The goods and services shown below are those we can provide our clients. You may choose only the items you desire. If legal or other requirements mean you must buy any item you do not specifically ask for, we will explain the reason in writing on the statement we provide describing the funeral goods and services you selected. When both casket and service are selected, we provide discounted choices for certain service selections. Prior to drafting any contract for goods and services, the responsible party of the decedents survivor who is handling funeral arrangements is entitled to receive a copy of any preneed agreement in the possession of the funeral establishment that has been signed and paid for, in full or in part, by or on behalf of the decedent.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICE FEE:

We charge a fee for our initial services. This fee is for our basic services and is already included with the price of our caskets. This same fee of $1,800 will be added to the cost of your funeral arrangements if you provide the casket. It includes such costs and services as availability for your initial inquiry and consultation, coordination and direction of Arrangements, Services and Disposition. Also included are the use of common facilities, equipment and necessary supplies for services and completion of the appropriate documents including California State registration of death, a county permit for disposition, and preparing of requests certified copies of the Original Certificate. No taxes, merchandise, limousine, cemetery or crematory fees or third party charges are included unless clearly specified.

SELECTED SERVICES

The discounted services listed here are specially packaged for your convenience and are available with purchase of both our services and the casket for use in services. With the initial professional services, we are available for your initial inquiry as well as for consultation, coordination and direction of Arrangements, Services and Disposition. Also included are the local ambulance call with attendant, sheltering and preparation of the remains as directed, the use of common facilities, equipment and necessary supplies for services and the completion of necessary documents including California State registration of death, the county permit for disposition and preparing requests for certified copies of the Original Certificate. Please note that items may be added to these ‘packaged’ selections, however substitutions and deletions are disallowed. No taxes or merchandise, limousine, cemetery, crematory or third-party charges are included unless clearly indicated.

RECEIVING REMAINS: $2,000

Provides with this selection is an ambulance to receiving depot, in addition to basic services and limited care of the remains prior to services, interment or cremation, and not including any unspecified services, California Permit filing fee, merchandise or disposal of outer shipping container.

DIRECT INTERMENT OR CREMATION: $1,820

This service includes appropriate documents and authorizations, local daytime ambulance, care and limited preparation and general care of remains. If you want to arrange a direct cremation, you can use an alternative container. Alternative containers encase the body and can be made of materials like fiberboard or composition materials (with or without outside covering); the container we provide is of fiberboard. In addition, as above including container for cremation: $1,945. Direct Interment including minimum burial container: $2,820. Pacemaker removal: additional cost.

DIRECT FORWARDING OF REMAINS: $2,980

Provides for local daytime ambulance, professional services, preparation of remains for transit, and local transport to Carrier’s Terminal, and does not include the shipping container, air tray or any sales tax due.

MEMORIAL SERVICE AT THE EVERGREEN MORTUARY: $3,020

In addition to the services included in direct interment or cremation as described above, this selection includes the use of one of our chapels or drawing rooms for either a Quiet Hour, visiting or a Memorial Service, with final interment scheduled at a separate time. Chapel time up to 2 hours.

GRAVESITE, COMMITTAL SERVICE OR WITNESS CREMATION*: $3,580

FUNERAL & VISITING AT CHURCH, CEMETERY OR OTHER LOCATION*: $4,280

Available with this service selection are staff and preparation appropriate to visiting, vigil and funeral services held in a local church, cemetery chapel or other selected location. In addition, visiting or a vigil in our chapel or drawing room for morning, afternoon or evening use may also be arranged. Church time up to 1 hour.

FUNERAL SERVICES AT THE EVERGREEN MORTUARY*: $4,520
