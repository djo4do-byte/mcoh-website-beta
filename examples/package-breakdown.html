How a package price is built up

Two examples, in receipt style. Every price and every line of wording below is taken from our General Price List. Where the price list does not break a price down, we say so instead of guessing.

For the owner’s review (remove before publishing)

  • “What this adds” is plain subtraction of two printed GPL package prices: $4,520 − $1,820 = $2,700, and $3,020 − $1,820 = $1,200. It is not a GPL line item.
  • Funeral Services: $2,700 matched the Professional Service Fee on the previous price list. The current price list shows that fee as $1,800, so the two no longer match. The GPL does not say how the package is built, so the page does not label it.
  • Memorial service: the GPL’s chapel time (up to 2.5 hours) is $1,160 and its memorial service in our parlor or chapel is $1,150. Neither equals the $1,200 difference, so no chapel line is added into the sum.
  • Package descriptions are the official wording, shown as printed (including “Provides with this selection is…” style phrasing).
