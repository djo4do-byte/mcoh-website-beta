Receiving and forwarding

Receiving of remains

When your loved one is coming home to San Francisco from elsewhere, we receive them into our care and coordinate with the sending funeral home.

Includes an ambulance to a receiving depot, our basic services, and limited care before services, interment or cremation. It does not include any unspecified services, the California permit filing fee, merchandise, or disposal of the outer shipping container.

Forwarding of remains

When your loved one is to be laid to rest outside the area, we prepare and forward them to the receiving funeral home.

Includes a local daytime ambulance, our professional services, preparation for transit, and local transport to the carrier’s terminal. It does not include the shipping container, air tray or any sales tax.
